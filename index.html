Personal automation

HubSoft STOCK Export

A private workflow that collects four footwear reports and saves one dated archive in a Google Drive folder selected by its owner.

How it works

  1. Sign into the owner's authorized Palladium Europe HubSoft account.
  2. Download Footwear reports 1, 2, 3 and 4, and validate the Excel files inside the ZIP.
  3. Upload the archive to the selected STOCK folder and check the uploaded file's size and checksum.

Google Drive access

The workflow requests Google's selected-file permission, drive.file. The owner chooses the destination folder during Google authorization. The workflow reads destination metadata, checks for duplicate exports, and creates report archives there.

This website provides information only. It does not sign into HubSoft, request Google authorization, or display report files.
